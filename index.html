Roomies Kenya: co‑living, made simple in Kileleshwa

Nairobi's young professionals and digital natives are choosing new ways to live. Co-living is gaining traction across the city because it's affordable, flexible and builds community at a time of rising rents and changing lifestyles.

At Roomies, we make that lifestyle accessible, without long-term commitments, hidden costs or isolation. Every room is furnished, every bill that matters is covered, and the shared spaces are cleaned for you.
